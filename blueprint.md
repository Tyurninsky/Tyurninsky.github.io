# **App Name**: Artisan Furnishings

## Core Features:

- Homepage Presentation: Showcase the brand's story, emphasizing the craftsmanship and unique qualities of handmade furniture, with a call to action to encourage custom orders.
- Product Catalog: Display furniture pieces categorized into chairs, tables, and cabinets.
- Product Detail Page: Present high-quality images, names, premium-style descriptions, and prices for each item. This page highlights the materials used and the craftsmanship involved.
- Master Craftsman Section: Detail the background of Nikolai, the craftsman, noting his 15 years of experience and his commitment to personalized, handmade furniture.
- Order Inquiry via Telegram: Enable potential customers to inquire about custom furniture orders directly through a Telegram link to @tyurninn.
- Contact Form: A form to capture contact details and requirements for custom furniture.
- AI-Powered Style Match: Users upload photos of their interiors and receive furniture recommendations matching their style. A tool helps to assess color palettes, design patterns and spatial dimensions in the images.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Warm and inviting brown (#A67B5B), reminiscent of natural wood and craftsmanship.
- Background color: Light beige (#F5F5DC), providing a neutral and clean canvas to highlight the furniture.
- Accent color: Deep copper (#B87333), used sparingly to draw attention to key elements and calls to action.
- Headline font: 'Playfair', serif, offering elegance and a high-end feel, perfect for headlines.
- Body font: 'PT Sans', sans-serif, ensuring readability and a modern touch for descriptions and general content. Paired with Playfair for the headlines.
- Use clean and minimalist icons to represent furniture categories and features, maintaining a handcrafted feel.
- Implement a grid-based layout with generous spacing to create a visually balanced and easy-to-navigate design.
